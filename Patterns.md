# Patterns

*Get design guidance for supporting common user actions, tasks, and experiences.*

- ![A sketch of a bar chart, suggesting data representation. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-charting-data-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Charting data](Patterns/Charting%20data.md) — Presenting data in a chart can help you communicate information with clarity and appeal.
- ![A sketch of a person with an overlapping checkmark, suggesting effective collaboration. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-collaboration-and-sharing-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Collaboration and sharing](Patterns/Collaboration%20and%20sharing.md) — Great collaboration and sharing experiences are simple and responsive, letting people engage with the content while communicating effectively with others.
- ![A sketch of two overlapping squares containing an arrow pointing to the upper-left, suggesting a transition to a new destination. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-drag-and-drop-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Drag and drop](Patterns/Drag%20and%20drop.md) — Using drag and drop, people can move or duplicate selected photos, text, and other content by dragging the selection from one location to another.
- ![A sketch of a pencil writing within a field, suggesting data entry. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-entering-data-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Entering data](Patterns/Entering%20data.md) — When you need information from people, design ways that make it easy for them to provide it without making mistakes.
- ![A sketch of a pointer surrounded by a circular set of short lines, suggesting a response to a mouse click. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-feedback-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Feedback](Patterns/Feedback.md) — Feedback helps people know what’s happening, discover what they can do next, understand the results of actions, and avoid mistakes.
- ![A sketch of a document with the upper right corner folded in, suggesting interaction with files. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-file-management-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [File management](Patterns/File%20management.md) — Some apps can support documents and files that people expect to manage throughout the system.
- ![A sketch of two outward-pointing arrows arranged in a vertical line extending from the upper-left to the bottom-right, suggesting expansion. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-going-full-screen-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Going full screen](Patterns/Going%20full%20screen.md) — iPhone, iPad, and Mac offer full-screen modes that let people expand a window to fill the screen, hiding system controls and providing a distraction-free environment.
- ![A sketch of a square containing an arrow pointing to the upper-right corner, suggesting a transition to a new state. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-launching-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Launching](Patterns/Launching.md) — A streamlined launch experience helps people start using your app or game immediately.
- ![A sketch of a television containing a play button, suggesting playback of media. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-live-viewing-apps-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Live-viewing apps](Patterns/Live-viewing%20apps.md) — As you design a live-viewing app, prioritize the content and create fun, fluid interactions that encourage immersion in the live-viewing experience.
- ![A sketch of a spinning indeterminate activity indicator, suggesting data loading. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-loading-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Loading](Patterns/Loading.md) — The best content-loading experience finishes before people become aware of it.
- ![A sketch of a person, suggesting personal information. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-managing-accounts-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Managing accounts](Patterns/Managing%20accounts.md) — When it doesn’t create an unnecessary barrier to your experience, an account can be a convenient way for people to access their content and track personal details.
- ![A sketch of bell with a small overlapping circle, suggesting a notification sound. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-managing-notifications-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Managing notifications](Patterns/Managing%20notifications.md) — Notifications can give people timely and important information, whether the device is locked or in use.
- ![A sketch of an active window above an inactive window, suggesting focus on the frontmost window. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-modality-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Modality](Patterns/Modality.md) — Modality is a design technique that presents content in a separate, dedicated mode that prevents interaction with the parent view and requires an explicit action to dismiss.
- ![A sketch of two side-by-side windows in a split view arrangement, suggesting multitasking. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-multitasking-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Multitasking](Patterns/Multitasking.md) — Multitasking lets people switch quickly from one app to another, performing tasks in each.
- ![A sketch of a question mark, suggesting help is available. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-offering-help-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Offering help](Patterns/Offering%20help.md) — Although the most effective experiences are approachable and intuitive, you can provide contextual help when necessary.
- ![A sketch of a waving hand, suggesting a gesture of welcoming. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-onboarding-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Onboarding](Patterns/Onboarding.md) — Onboarding can help people get a quick start using your app or game.
- ![A sketch of a speaker emitting sound waves, suggesting the playback of audio. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-playing-audio-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Playing audio](Patterns/Playing%20audio.md) — People expect rich audio experiences that automatically adjust when the context changes on the device.
- ![A sketch of a horizontal line of three slightly overlapping circles, suggesting vibration. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-playing-haptics-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Playing haptics](Patterns/Playing%20haptics.md) — Playing haptics can engage people’s sense of touch and bring their familiarity with the physical world into your app or game.
- ![A sketch of a play button, suggesting video playback. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-playing-video-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Playing video](Patterns/Playing%20video.md) — People expect to enjoy rich video experiences on their devices, regardless of the app or game they’re using.
- ![A sketch of a printer, suggesting printing. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-printing-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Printing](Patterns/Printing.md) — An iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or visionOS app can integrate system-provided print functionality when it makes sense, presenting custom printer- and document-specific options if necessary.
- ![A sketch of a half-filled star, suggesting a favorability rating. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-ratings-and-reviews-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Ratings and reviews](Patterns/Ratings%20and%20reviews.md) — People often view the ratings and reviews for an app or game before they download it.
- ![A sketch of a magnifying glass, suggesting the search for information. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-searching-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Searching](Patterns/Searching.md) — People use various search techniques to find content on their device, within an app, and within a document or file.
- ![A sketch of a gear, suggesting configuration. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-settings-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Settings](Patterns/Settings.md) — People expect apps and games to just work, but they also appreciate having ways to customize the experience to fit their needs.
- ![A sketch of an arrow that starts right, curves upward, and points to the left, suggesting a return to the start. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-undo-and-redo-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Undo and redo](Patterns/Undo%20and%20redo.md) — Undo and redo gives people easy ways to reverse many types of actions, which can also help people explore and experiment safely as they learn a new interface or task.
- ![A sketch of a person running, suggesting exercise. The image is tinted orange to subtly reflect the orange in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/patterns-workouts-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Workouts](Patterns/Workouts.md) — A great workout or fitness experience encourages people to engage with their current activity and helps them track their progress on their devices.
