# Inputs

*Learn about the various methods people use to control your app or game and enter data.*

- ![A sketch of an arrow pointing toward the Action button on Apple Watch, suggesting initiating an action. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-action-button-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Action button](Inputs/Action%20button.md) — The Action button gives people quick access to their favorite features on supported iPhone and Apple Watch models.
- ![A sketch of a scribble mark, suggesting drawing with Apple Pencil. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-apple-pencil-and-scribble-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Apple Pencil and Scribble](Inputs/Apple%20Pencil%20and%20Scribble.md) — Apple Pencil helps make drawing, handwriting, and marking effortless and natural, in addition to performing well as a pointer and UI interaction tool.
- ![A sketch of an arrow pointing toward the Camera Control on iPhone, suggesting initiating an action. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-camera-control-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Camera Control](Inputs/Camera%20Control.md) — The Camera Control provides direct access to your app’s camera experience.
- ![A sketch of a curved arrow beside a Digital Crown, that suggests turning the Digital Crown. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-digital-crown-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Digital Crown](Inputs/Digital%20Crown.md) — The Digital Crown is an important hardware input for Apple Vision Pro and Apple Watch.
- ![A sketch of a human eye. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-eyes-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Eyes](Inputs/Eyes.md) — In visionOS, people look at a virtual object to identify it as a target they can interact with.
- ![A sketch of a frame around a circular interface element, suggesting locking focus on an object. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-focus-and-selection-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Focus and selection](Inputs/Focus%20and%20selection.md) — Focus helps people visually confirm the object that their interaction targets.
- ![A sketch of a D-pad control from a game controller, suggesting gameplay. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-game-controls-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Game controls](Inputs/Game%20controls.md) — Precise, intuitive game controls enhance gameplay and can increase a player’s immersion in the game.
- ![A sketch of a pointing hand swiping in a curved motion toward the right, suggesting touch interaction with a device. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-gestures-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Gestures](Inputs/Gestures.md) — A gesture is a physical motion that a person uses to directly affect an object in an app or game on their device.
- ![A sketch of a gyroscope, suggesting movement. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-gyro-and-accelerometer-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Gyroscope and accelerometer](Inputs/Gyroscope%20and%20accelerometer.md) — On-device gyroscopes and accelerometers can supply data about a device’s movement in the physical world.
- ![A sketch of a keyboard, suggesting keyboard input. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-keyboards-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Keyboards](Inputs/Keyboards.md) — A physical keyboard can be an essential input device for entering text, playing games, controlling apps, and more.
- ![A sketch of curved lines beside a circular area containing a smaller circle, suggesting audio approaching a person in a room from a specific direction. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-nearby-interactions-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Nearby interactions](Inputs/Nearby%20interactions.md) — Nearby interactions support on-device experiences that integrate the presence of people and objects in the nearby environment.
- ![A sketch of an arrow-shaped pointer, suggesting use of a mouse or trackpad. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-pointing-devices-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Pointing devices](Inputs/Pointing%20devices.md) — People can use a pointing device like a trackpad or mouse to navigate the interface and initiate actions.
- ![A sketch of an Apple TV remote, suggesting interaction with onscreen content. The image is tinted purple to subtly reflect the purple in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/inputs-remotes-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Remotes](Inputs/Remotes.md) — The Siri Remote is the primary input method for Apple TV, helping people feel connected to onscreen content from across the room.
