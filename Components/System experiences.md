# System experiences

- ![A sketch of the Shortcuts icon. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-app-shortcuts-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [App Shortcuts](System%20experiences/App%20Shortcuts.md) — An App Shortcut gives people access to your app’s key functions or content throughout the system.
- ![A stylized representation of an Apple Watch face that includes the time and a set of differently sized complications. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-complications-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Complications](System%20experiences/Complications.md) — A complication displays timely, relevant information on the watch face, where people can view it each time they raise their wrist.
- ![A stylized representation of empty controls in Control Center that are different sizes. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-controls-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Controls](System%20experiences/Controls.md) — A control provides quick access to a feature of your app from Control Center, the Lock Screen, or the Action button.
- ![A stylized representation of the Dynamic Island, in collapsed form, displaying the score of a live sporting event. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-live-activities-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Live Activities](System%20experiences/Live%20Activities.md) — A Live Activity lets people track the progress of an activity, event, or task at a glance.
- ![A stylized representation of a notification mockup. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-notifications-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Notifications](System%20experiences/Notifications.md) — A notification gives people timely, high-value information they can understand at a glance.
- ![A sketch of a snippet. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-snippets-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Snippets](System%20experiences/Snippets.md) — When someone performs a task with Siri or an App Shortcut, a snippet shows the result or asks for confirmation.
- ![A stylized representation of an iPhone status bar showing the time and cellular, Wi-Fi, and battery levels. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-status-bars-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Status bars](System%20experiences/Status%20bars.md) — A status bar appears along the upper edge of the screen and displays information about the device’s current state, like the time, cellular carrier, and battery level.
- ![A stylized representation of a horizontal list of media previews above a row of Apple TV apps. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-top-shelf-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Top Shelf](System%20experiences/Top%20Shelf.md) — The Apple TV Home Screen provides an area called Top Shelf, which showcases your content in a rich, engaging way while also giving people access to their favorite apps in the Dock.
- ![A stylized representation of a series of Apple Watch faces. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-watch-faces-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Watch faces](System%20experiences/Watch%20faces.md) — A watch face is a view that people choose as their primary view in watchOS.
- ![A stylized representation of a set of different-sized widgets on an iPad Home Screen. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-widgets-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Widgets](System%20experiences/Widgets.md) — A widget provides quick access to essential information and focused interactions from your app or game in additional contexts.
