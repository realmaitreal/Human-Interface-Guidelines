# Navigation and search

- ![A stylized representation of a path control for a document showing its parent folder. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-path-controls-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Path controls](Navigation%20and%20search/Path%20controls.md) — A path control shows the file system path of a selected file or folder.
- ![A stylized representation of a search field containing placeholder text and a dictation icon. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-search-fields-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Search fields](Navigation%20and%20search/Search%20fields.md) — A search field lets people search a collection of content for specific terms they enter.
- ![A stylized representation of a portion of a window's sidebar displaying a section and some folders. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-sidebars-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Sidebars](Navigation%20and%20search/Sidebars.md) — A sidebar appears on the leading side of a view and lets people navigate between areas of your app or top-level collections of content, like folders and playlists.
- ![A stylized representation of a tab bar containing three placeholder icons with names. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-tab-bars-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Tab bars](Navigation%20and%20search/Tab%20bars.md) — A tab bar lets people navigate between top-level sections of your app.
- ![A stylized representation of a text field containing tokens for prefix and number values. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-token-fields-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Token fields](Navigation%20and%20search/Token%20fields.md) — A token field is a type of text field that can convert text into *tokens* that are easy to select and manipulate.
