# Menus and actions

- ![A stylized representation of a share button. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-activity-views-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Activity views](Menus%20and%20actions/Activity%20views.md) — An activity view — often called a *share sheet* — presents a range of tasks that people can perform in the current context.
- ![A stylized representation of a button. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-buttons-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Buttons](Menus%20and%20actions/Buttons.md) — A button initiates an instantaneous action.
- ![A stylized representation of a contextual menu beneath a clicking pointer. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-context-menus-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Context menus](Menus%20and%20actions/Context%20menus.md) — A context menu provides access to functionality that’s directly related to an item, without cluttering the interface.
- ![A stylized representation of a menu extending from an icon in the Dock. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-dock-menus-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Dock menus](Menus%20and%20actions/Dock%20menus.md) — On a Mac, people can secondary click an app’s or game’s icon in the Dock to reveal a Dock menu, which presents both system-provided and custom items.
- ![A stylized representation of an edit menu. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-edit-menus-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Edit menus](Menus%20and%20actions/Edit%20menus.md) — An edit menu lets people make changes to selected content in the current view, in addition to offering related commands like Copy, Select, Translate, and Look Up.
- ![A stylized representation of a set of menu items extending up from an app icon. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-home-screen-quick-actions-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Home Screen quick actions](Menus%20and%20actions/Home%20Screen%20quick%20actions.md) — Home Screen quick actions give people a way to perform app-specific actions from the Home Screen.
- ![A stylized representation of a menu containing a selected item and displaying a submenu. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-menus-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Menus](Menus%20and%20actions/Menus.md) — A menu reveals its options when people interact with it, making it a space-efficient way to present commands in your app or game.
- ![A stylized representation of an ornament at the bottom of a window. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-ornaments-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Ornaments](Menus%20and%20actions/Ornaments.md) — In visionOS, an ornament presents controls and information related to a window, without crowding or obscuring the window’s contents.
- ![A stylized representation of a pop-up button containing a selected option. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-pop-up-buttons-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Pop-up buttons](Menus%20and%20actions/Pop-up%20buttons.md) — A pop-up button displays a menu of mutually exclusive options.
- ![A stylized representation of a pull-down menu containing a selected item. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-pull-down-buttons-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Pull-down buttons](Menus%20and%20actions/Pull-down%20buttons.md) — A pull-down button displays a menu of items or actions that directly relate to the button’s purpose.
- ![A stylized representation of the macOS menu bar displaying a selected menu. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-the-menu-bar-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [The menu bar](Menus%20and%20actions/The%20menu%20bar.md) — On a Mac or an iPad, the menu bar at the top of the screen displays the top-level menus in your app or game.
- ![A stylized representation of top and bottom toolbars. The image is tinted red to subtly reflect the red in the original six-color Apple logo.](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/components-toolbars-thumbnail@2x.png)  
  [Toolbars](Menus%20and%20actions/Toolbars.md) — A toolbar provides convenient access to frequently used commands, controls, navigation, and search.
